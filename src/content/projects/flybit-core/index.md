## Overview

**Flybit Engine** is an ultra-fast static web engine and publishing architecture engineered specifically for technical writers, systems engineers, and research teams who demand a zero-friction, zero-bloat content pipeline.

### Highlights
- **Pure SSG**: Pre-renders to 100% static HTML at build time, eliminating runtime bottlenecks.
- **Obsidian Native**: Direct page-bundle integration with local Markdown vaults.
- **Four Tailored Palettes**: Slate Navy, Clean White, Warm Paper, and Midnight OLED.
- **Accessible & SEO-Engineered**: Complete Schema.org JSON-LD, semantic markup, and native browser speech narration.
